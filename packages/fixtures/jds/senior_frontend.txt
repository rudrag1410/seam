Senior Frontend Engineer at Razorpay

About the role
Join our checkout team to build the fastest, most reliable payment experience in India. This is a full-time, hybrid role based in Bengaluru. Salary range: INR 3,000,000 to 4,500,000 per year.

Responsibilities
- Own the performance of high-traffic checkout flows
- Collaborate with design and backend engineers to ship new features
- Mentor engineers and raise the quality bar across the frontend codebase

Requirements
- 4+ years of experience building production web applications with React and TypeScript
- Strong understanding of web performance, including Core Web Vitals
- Experience with Next.js and server-side rendering
- Experience with automated testing (Jest, Cypress or Playwright)
- Experience building or contributing to a design system with Storybook
- Working knowledge of GraphQL APIs

Nice to have
- Experience with Kubernetes or AWS
- Open-source contributions
- Accessibility (WCAG) experience
